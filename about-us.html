About Us

A Strong Heritage. A Brighter Tomorrow.

Thirupathy Bright Industries (Thirupathy Bright Industries) is a Chennai-based manufacturer and exporter of bright steel bars and engineered components, with a manufacturing legacy spanning over three decades.

About Thirupathy Bright Industries

Engineering Since 1996

Established in 1996, Thirupathy Bright Industries has developed specialised capabilities in cold drawing, wire drawing, peeling, centreless grinding and machining, enabling us to manufacture steel products to specific customer requirements.

Located at Gummidipoondi, near Chennai, our facility benefits from proximity to major automotive and engineering manufacturing clusters as well as ports, supporting both domestic and international supply.

We manufacture bright bars in a wide range of grades, sizes, shapes and customised profiles, serving applications across automotive, engineering, manufacturing and related industrial sectors.
