Quality Testing

In-House Testing Capability

Thirupathy Bright Industries maintains in-house testing capabilities to support inspection and quality verification throughout the manufacturing process.

Spectro Analysis

Spectrometer-based chemical composition analysis for incoming raw material and finished bars.

Hardness Analysis

Brinell and Rockwell hardness testing to verify mechanical properties.

Tensile Analysis

Universal Testing Machine (UTM) analysis for tensile strength and elongation.

Surface Crack Testing

Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI) to detect surface and near-surface defects.

Internal Crack Testing

Ultrasonic testing to identify internal defects not visible on the surface.

Sample Preparation

Careful polishing of test samples ahead of metallurgical examination.
