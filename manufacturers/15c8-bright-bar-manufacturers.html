What is the chemical composition of 15C8?

15C8 (Mild Steel) typically contains C 0.10 – 0.20%; Mn 0.60 – 0.90%; Si 0.15 – 0.35%; P ≤ 0.050%; S ≤ 0.050%.

What is the minimum size available?

Minimum sizes: bright (cold drawn) rounds from 3 mm, squares from 4.75 mm, hexagons from 8 mm AF and flats from 12x5 mm (width x thickness); black (hot rolled) rounds from 5.5 mm, squares from 10 mm and flats from 20x6 mm. Other sizes can be supplied subject to MOQ and raw material availability.

What sizes are available?

Bright (cold drawn): rounds 3–101.6 mm, squares 4.75–78.5 mm, hexagons 8–100 mm AF, flats 12×5–150×50 mm (W×T). Black (hot rolled): rounds 5.5–200 mm, squares 10–250 mm, flats 20×6–350×80 mm (W×T).
